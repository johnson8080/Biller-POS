# Voice-over script - Biller POS advertisement

The film runs **1 minute 51 seconds**. Each line is one file in `audio/`
(`line-00.wav` to `line-14.wav`). Record them one by one, in this order, and
drop them into the `audio/` folder, then run `node record-ad.js`.

Keep a short pause at the end of each line - the film re-times itself from the
length of the files, so long pauses make the film long.

---

## 1 - Opening (about 6 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Hii ni Biller POS. Duka yako lote, inaendeshwa kwa simu moja.

**English**
> This is Biller POS. Your whole shop, running from one phone.

---

## 2 - Selling (about 6 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Bidhaa zote ziko kwenye skrini moja pamoja na bei zao, hivyo unauza kwa sekunde.

**English**
> Every item sits on one screen with its price, so you sell in seconds.

---

## 3 - Scanning (about 7 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Scan barcode, kama sokomasi. Bidhaa huingia moja kwa moja kwenye kadi ya mteja.

**English**
> Scan the barcode, just like in a supermarket. The item goes straight into the cart.

---

## 4 - Scanned (about 6 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Imescanwa, imehesabiwa, imeongezeka. Hakuna kuchukua na mkono.

**English**
> Scanned, priced and added. No typing, no mistakes.

---

## 5 - Search (about 3 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Tafuta bidhaa yoyote haraka.

**English**
> Search for anything in an instant.

---

## 6 - The bill (about 7 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Mteja anaona jumla, wewe unajua kubadilisha na faida yako kabla ya kukaza pesa.

**English**
> The customer sees the total, and you know the change and your profit before you take the money.

---

## 7 - M-Pesa (about 7 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Pokea M-Pesa moja kwa moja. Andika namba ya mteja na tuma ombi.

**English**
> Take M-Pesa straight from the till. Type the customer's number and send the request.

---

## 8 - The PIN prompt (about 7 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Simu yake inapata ombi la kuingia PIN ya M-Pesa. Subiri mpaka atakubali.

**English**
> Their phone gets the usual M-Pesa PIN prompt. You wait while they approve it.

---

## 9 - The receipt (about 6 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Risiti inabeba M-Pesa code, hivyo kila malipo huwezi kufuatiliwa.

**English**
> The receipt keeps the M-Pesa code, so every payment is easy to trace.

---

## 10 - Stock (about 6 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Jua kilicho dukani. Stock ikiwa kidogo unajua kabla ya kuisha.

**English**
> Know what is on the shelf. Low stock is flagged before you run out.

---

## 11 - Reports (about 7 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Tazama mapato ya leo na faida, cash, M-Pesa na deni pamoja.

**English**
> See today's sales and profit, with cash, M-Pesa and credit side by side.

---

## 12 - Customer debt (about 6 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Kumbuka madeni ya kila mteja, na ulipe kwa M-Pesa.

**English**
> Remember what each customer owes, and settle a debt with M-Pesa too.

---

## 13 - Your shop, your look (about 8 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Chagua rangi na ukubwa wa maandishi. Duka lako linaendelea kwenye akaunti yako, kwenye kila simu.

**English**
> Choose your colours and text size. Your shop stays in your account, on every phone.

---

## 14 - The plan (about 7 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Mipango moja: mia sita shilingi kwa mwezi, na ukikumbusha kabla ya kulipwa.

**English**
> One plan: six hundred shillings a month, with a reminder before it is due.

---

## 15 - Every sale is recorded (about 7 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Kila muuzaji umeandikwa, na unakuta kwa kipeo.

**English**
> Every sale is recorded, and you can find it again.

---

## 16 - The closing (about 14 seconds)

**Kiswahili**
> Tunakusaidia kuanza: tunaskana bidhaa zako na kuzihesabia kwenye stock. Tuma ujumbe kwa WhatsApp, sifuri saba mbili, nne, sifuri sita, tatu mbili.

**English**
> We also help you get started, scanning your items and adding them to stock. Message us on WhatsApp, zero seven two four, zero six seven, six three two.
